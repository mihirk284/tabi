**Venue**: Autonomous Robots, 2022.

### Abstract
This work presents an autonomous system for mapping and spectroscopic analysis of distributed radiation fields using aerial robots. The approach enables safe and efficient characterization of radioactive environments, with applications in nuclear facility inspection, environmental monitoring, and emergency response scenarios.
