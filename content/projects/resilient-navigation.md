**Authors**: P. De Petris, H. Nguyen, M. Dharmadhikari, M. Kulkarni, S. Zimmermann, and K. Alexis.

**Venue**: IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2021.

### Abstract
This work presents a resilient collision-tolerant navigation approach for aerial robots operating in confined environments. The method enables robust autonomous flight in challenging scenarios where occasional collisions with the environment may occur, making it particularly suitable for subterranean exploration and industrial inspection tasks.
