**Venue**: International Conference on Unmanned Aircraft Systems (ICUAS) 2023.

### Abstract
This work presents a method for manhole detection and traversal enabling micro aerial vehicles to explore ballast water tanks in marine vessels. The approach allows autonomous navigation through particularly narrow manholes connecting tank compartments, which is critical for comprehensive inspection of vessel safety-critical structures.
