### Overview
This book chapter provides a comprehensive overview of aerial field robotics, covering the design, control, and deployment of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) for real-world field applications. Topics include autonomous navigation, perception, and the unique challenges of operating aerial robots in unstructured outdoor environments.
