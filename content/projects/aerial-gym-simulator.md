[**Aerial Gym Simulator**](https://github.com/ntnu-arl/aerial_gym_simulator) is a framework for highly parallelized simulation of aerial robots. It leverages NVIDIA Isaac Gym to enable training of complex RL policies for vision-based navigation and control in record time.

#### [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/ntnu-arl/aerial_gym_simulator) • [Project Website](https://ntnu-arl.github.io/aerial_gym_simulator) {.centered-text}

Highlights:
- Parallel simulation of thousands of drones.
- GPU-accelerated sensing and physics.
- Seamless integration with PyTorch for RL training.
